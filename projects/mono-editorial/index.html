Room I — Preface

Wall text, beside the door

This catalogue documents five canvases that do not exist until you look at them. Each is painted by a small machine that has studied the light at Giverny — the gold of haystacks at dusk, the green hush of the lily pond — and left to work, generously imperfect, forever.

— The Keeper, Galerie Plein Airest. 2026

Manifesto — §1

A painting is never finished, only abandoned in good light. We build pages the way the plein-air painters built canvases: outdoors, in weather, with pigment still wet when the visitor arrives.

Hours

Dawn to dusk,
and after dark by firefly.
The garden paints through the night.

Admission

Free,
as air and light should be.
Donations of attention accepted.

Address

Galerie Plein Air,
the browser you are holding,
wherever the light is good.

Take the
light with you.
