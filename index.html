Artist
Statement

I paint the way the Impressionists worked — outdoors, in changing light, chasing weather — but my brush is a generative system and my pigment is noise.

Naturalism gives me the subject: wind, water, grass, dusk. Impressionism gives me the method: loose strokes, layered washes, light before detail. Code gives me the one thing neither had — a canvas that keeps breathing after the painting is finished.

Nothing here is photographed or prerecorded; every world runs whether or not anyone is watching.
